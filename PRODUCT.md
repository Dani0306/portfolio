# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Companies hiring a developer**: recruiters and engineering leads deciding in under a minute whether Daniel is worth an interview. They scan for availability, real shipped work and how he works.
- **Upwork clients**: small businesses and founders looking for AI automation, a full stack developer or a virtual assistant who can make their processes faster with AI tools.

## Product Purpose

The personal site of Daniel Andrés Colorado Sánchez, a full stack developer in Medellín, Colombia, available now for remote, hybrid, on site or relocation work. Success means a visitor understands what he builds, believes it through real projects and code, and gets in touch.

## Positioning

AI Automation Specialist and Full Stack Developer. He directs Claude Code with written context, tools (MCP servers, skills, hooks) and a review on every change, and uses it to build apps, automate processes and turn ideas into products that ship. Two years of customer support and back office work (Zara through Konecta; Third Way Health, managing patient prescription workflows) give him the operations side that virtual assistant and automation clients need.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive from job applications, LinkedIn and Upwork, on desktop and phone.
- The site is bilingual: English at `/`, Spanish at `/es/`.
- Time zone COT (UTC−5), which overlaps US working hours.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Astro static site on Vercel at danielcolorado.dev; tests run in GitHub Actions (Vitest, Playwright in Chromium and WebKit, a WCAG AA contrast check).
- The scroll world uses existing videos and stills only. No new images or video are generated.
- Project screenshots are real captures of the live sites, never generated.
- The résumé is a generated one page PDF in English.

## Brand Commitments

- Motto: "You imagine. I build. It ships." (Spanish: "Tú lo imaginas. Yo lo construyo. Sale a producción.")
- Copy avoids hyphens inside sentences and AI cliches.

## Evidence on Hand

- Four live projects with case studies: Classly, Yield, Forest Media Récords, Grupo Autos Colorado (`src/content/projects`, `src/content/projects-es`).
- Their public repos, including each project's CLAUDE.md, Classly's Supabase MCP server, Yield's pg_cron reminders and Forest's decision notes, skills and hooks.
- This site's own automation: résumé and share card builders, contrast check, CI.
- GitHub contribution graph, read at build time.
- Absences: no testimonials, client logos, metrics or employer details beyond the CV. Future projects may appear only when clearly labeled as not shipped yet ("In the lab").

## Product Principles

1. Every claim is backed by something a visitor can open: a live site, a repo or a file.
2. Work not shipped yet is labeled as such, never presented as done.
3. Lead with availability and the fastest way to get in touch.
4. Show how he works, not just what he used.
