## The client

Grupo Autos Colorado is a family car dealership in Colombia that buys and sells vehicles. The site has one job: show the catalog, and get a buyer talking to the dealership on WhatsApp as fast as possible.

## What I built

A one page site in Spanish: a hero, the company's story and vision, the vehicle catalog with a button to open the full list, and a WhatsApp button that starts the conversation. The vehicles come from a typed data file, so adding a car means adding one entry. Sections animate in as they scroll into view, through a small shared hook over `react-intersection-observer`.

## How it went

Four days of work in March 2026 and sixteen commits. Many of them are layout fixes found by checking the site on a phone: spacing, the hero image on small screens, the navbar on scroll and the gaps in the catalog grid.
