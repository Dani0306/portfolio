# Home page: AI section

Scope: a new section, "How I build with AI", on the home page (`src/components/sections/AI.astro`), placed right after the scroll world and before Selected work, plus the scroll world's "How I work" stop reworked into its teaser. Visitor mode: Experience, extending the incumbent world (dark editor, amber accent, file tabs, line gutters, deploy log). No new visual identity.

Audience: hiring companies and Upwork clients, equally.

## Direction contract

THESIS: AI as a team Daniel directs, proven with his real files. Refuses the category default of icon cards listing AI tools and buzzwords.

OWN-WORLD: the site's editor world: canvas #0e0f12, ink #ece8df, amber accent, Geist with JetBrains Mono only for code, paths and status. File tab bars, line number gutters, hairline borders, review comments pinned to lines.

STORY: the visitor sees how he gives AI context, tools and rules, how that builds apps, automates processes and turns ideas into products, what is in the lab next, then gets in touch.

FIRST VIEWPORT: heading and lead on the left; the workbench on the right, an editor pane showing a real file from one of his repos with review comments pinned to specific lines.

SIGNATURE: the workbench. Three jobs (build apps, automate processes, ideas into products) switch the open file with the Work section's clip wipe; each comment lights its line.

PROOF: verbatim excerpts with real line numbers and GitHub links; lab concepts labeled "In the lab", never shown as shipped.

seed: none (extension of an established surface; no concept roll)
